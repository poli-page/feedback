# Contributing

Thanks for helping make Poli Page better. This repo collects bug reports, feature requests, and questions for the editor, the dashboard, the documentation site, and the website. Issues specific to the CLI, SDKs, framework integrations or the templates collection belong on the matching public repo (see the [README](README.md#what-this-repo-is-not) for the full list).

## How to report a bug

1. Use the [bug report template](https://github.com/poli-page/feedback/issues/new?template=bug.yml).
2. Give a clear repro: minimal steps we can follow on our side.
3. Attach screenshots or a short screen recording when relevant - it usually saves a round-trip.
4. Indicate the **editor version** (`Help > About`) and your **OS**. If you triggered the form from the editor's in-app "Report a bug" command, both fields are pre-filled for you.

The more reproducible the report, the faster we can ship a fix.

## How to suggest a feature

1. Use the [feature request template](https://github.com/poli-page/feedback/issues/new?template=feature.yml).
2. **Start with the problem**, not the solution. What were you trying to do? What slowed you down or felt wrong? The "why" matters more than the "what".
3. Then describe the solution you have in mind.
4. Mention alternatives you have considered or workarounds you currently use.

## How to ask a question

Prefer [Discussions](https://github.com/poli-page/feedback/discussions) (Q&A category) over Issues for questions. It keeps the issue tracker focused on actionable work, and your question becomes searchable for other users.

Use the question issue template only if you suspect a documentation gap or want the question tracked alongside bugs.

## Code of conduct

Be respectful, assume good intent, no hate speech, no spam. Maintainers may close or lock threads at their discretion.

## Privacy

We may quote your issue or discussion publicly when discussing improvements, in changelogs, or in release notes.

**Do not paste sensitive data** (API keys, customer data, internal URLs, personal information, etc.) in issues or discussions. If you need to share something private to reproduce a bug, email `contact@poli.page` instead.
